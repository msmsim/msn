“How you do anything is how you do everything” applies to AI use in creative production, too. If, pre-AI, someone conscientiously crafted every part of the whole, the same will happen now. If one satisficed to simply to get to an output or outcome that mattered, that will still continue. If someone mindlessly spammed with less than zero regard for the recipients, that too will persist.

The technology feels mature enough for production use, and for my kind of writing at least, no longer needs special treatment or expectations-setting that is different in any way. The default has shifted — it is now fully hand-written work that is exceptional. But I don’t flag that either.
