Pivot to fat waste, not lean value.

Yes, obviously tokens/intelligence is the thing to waste, but the key idea is to pursue waste rather than utility. Silliness, triviality, self-indulgence, absurdity — these are the signals to follow in deciding how to use your $20/mo worth of tokens (or whatever your spend level is — $20 is already enough to waste). This is much harder than it looks. I keep getting distracted from my waste projects by utility projects. This is true even for big corps suddenly hit with millions of dollars of bills. That’s not true waste but inefficient pursuit of value. You want true waste. Which means the goals have to be somewhat silly.
