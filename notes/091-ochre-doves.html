Imagine one’s capacity for movement as a fire. The correct way to maintain the fire is to treat it like a wood burner or hearth, tending to it continuously, little and often, basking in the literal and communal ambience, and making use of it for recurring daily events. The incorrect way is to treat it like a bonfire, piling up detritus and every once in a while pumping the blaze to a great intensity before letting it burn out again.
