When I say that our society is becoming more sedentary, I don’t mean we’re exercising less. I mean that we’re using more and more technologies that reduce the number of purposeful human movements.

Technology is capable of deepening our embodiment. Whether it actually ends up doing that is another matter. Inconvenience is generative in its own way and we don't realise that until we've zapped all of it from our lives.
