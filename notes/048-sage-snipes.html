Perception without action; information without decision; energy without expenditure. Many acute issues can be mapped to a growing supply of X without a proportional, demand-matched and structurally unobstructed discharge to Y. Which provides some simple (but not easy) solutions:

  • Too much X? Reduce supply.
  • X to wrong Y? Redirect flow.
  • Y blocked? Remove impediment.
